# FIT0450 manufacturer STEP source

The owner supplied the original `FIT0450 Solidworks File.zip`, containing **`FIT0450.STEP`**.

The STEP was successfully opened as a 13-solid assembly with overall model bounding dimensions approximately **29.86 × 44.39 × 75.48 mm** in its original coordinate orientation. These values differ from the simplified catalog envelope of 55 × 48 × 24 mm, so CAD fitting must use the actual STEP orientation and clearances, not just the catalog bounding box.

- Source STEP: manufacturer supplied
- Original FreeCAD chassis: available
- Previous holders: provisional and should not be described as FIT0450-fit-verified
- Next design requirement: align the motor output shaft to the wheel axis, avoid the encoder and cable exits, and perform an interference test with the complete 13-solid model

The manufacturer STEP is included in the locally prepared design package; redistribution of the manufacturer's model in GitHub should respect its original terms.
